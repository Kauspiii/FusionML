  First call (incl. compile): 661.1 ms
  ANE steady-state:  median=22.325 ms  std=4.319 ms
  CPU (Accelerate):  median=51.941 ms
  GPU (MLX):         median=7.871 ms

  ANE/CPU ratio: 0.43x (ANE faster)

======================================================================
  VERDICT
======================================================================
  ANE is SLOWER than GPU for every tested shape, even at steady-state
  (model compiled and cached). Adding ANE to the model-level scheduler
  would not help for these matmul sizes on this hardware.
